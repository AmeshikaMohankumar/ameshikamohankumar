About Me

I believe great digital experiences occur at the crossroads of rigorous user empathy and clean, high-performance code.

I’m an IT graduate and UI/UX designer who turns product ideas into simple, intuitive journeys. I plan user flows, design and prototype in Figma, then build responsive pages with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. At Gamage Recruiters I designed web and mobile interfaces for real client briefs, and I use AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT to prototype and test ideas faster while keeping accessibility in mind.
